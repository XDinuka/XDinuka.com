Spec

§01

Automatic transaction tracking

Every debit or purchase alert is captured instantly. KoSalli also reads your existing SMS inbox, so your full history is there from day one.

§02

Major Sri Lankan bank support

KoSalli understands SMS formats from eight major banks, covering both credit and debit cards.

DFCC Bank Commercial Bank Peoples Bank BOC HNB Sampath Bank Pan Asia Bank NTB
§03

Smart auto-categorization

Set an exact rule ("KEELLS → Groceries") or a keyword rule ("PIZZA → Dining") and KoSalli applies it to every matching transaction — past and future. Ranked keyword rules keep you in control.

§04

Categories & budgets

Set optional monthly and/or weekly spending limits per category, with a daily notification if you go over. Merge or delete categories freely — their transactions stay, just uncategorized.

§05

Bill tracking

Electricity, water, mobile, and credit card bills are picked up automatically from SMS, marked paid or unpaid, with a reminder a configurable number of days before each is due.

§06

Fuel Pass tracking

Built for Sri Lanka's National Fuel Pass system: see your weekly quota remaining per vehicle, whether today is your eligible fill-up day, and get a reminder before the week resets.

§07

Home screen at a glance

See today's and this month's spending the moment you open the app, with your full transaction list right there — search, filter, sort, and bulk-edit whenever you need to.

§08

Spending review

Break down your spending by category for any time period and see exactly where your money goes.

§09

Home screen widget

Add a resizable widget showing a single metric of your choice — total spending, a category's spending, budget remaining, or an account balance — for today, this week, or this month.

§10

Scheduled spending summaries

Opt in to a daily, weekly, or monthly notification that delivers a spending summary on whatever day and time suits you.

Your data stays on your phone.

KoSalli works completely offline. All data is stored in a local database on your device and never sent to any server — no third parties, no advertisers, no analytics.
